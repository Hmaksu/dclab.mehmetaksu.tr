DocVerify Introduced

Construction Site Photo Validation & Tamper Detection Software

Software & Tool Published: August 2026

Project Overview

Developed by researchers at the Digital Construction Laboratory (DCL), DocVerify is an advanced decision-support tool engineered to verify the authenticity and reliability of inspection photos taken at construction sites during quality control and progress monitoring.

Designed to eliminate risks of digital image manipulation, false timestamp/location tags, and fraud, the system executes AI-driven pixel-level analysis and cross-validates EXIF metadata against spatial site logs.

Key Technical Capabilities

  • Pixel Manipulation Detection: Identifying digital montages, retouching, and object removal using deep neural network architectures.
  • EXIF & Location Verification: Cross-checking photo capture date, time, camera settings, and GPS coordinates against project schedule boundaries.
  • BIM Integration: Aligning site progress photos directly with Building Information Models (BIM) to automatically flag discrepancies.
