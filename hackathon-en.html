European Researchers' Night Hackathon

Marie Skłodowska-Curie Actions BBGNight-TR & ITU WP2 Hackathon Event

Hackathon & Event Event: BBGNight-TR MSCA European Researchers' Night

Event Highlights

Supported by the European Commission Marie Skłodowska-Curie Actions, the DCL team led and coordinated the ITU WP2 Hackathon during the European Researchers' Night (BBGNight-TR) initiative.

Young engineering researchers and computer science students participated in an intensive 24-hour sprint focused on construction sustainability, AI-driven jobsite automation, and digital twin analytics.

Mentorship & Outcomes

Laboratory researchers provided technical mentorship in algorithm design, data processing, and pitching methodologies. Winning teams were awarded certificates and academic collaboration support.
