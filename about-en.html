About Us

Conducting scientific research and pioneering solutions at the intersection of civil engineering and digital technologies.

Our Vision

To be a landmark research laboratory contributing to the digital transformation of the construction industry on an international scale through cutting-edge research in artificial intelligence, digital twins, and advanced data analytics, bridging academia, industry, and public institutions.

Our Mission

To develop AI-driven, data-based solutions for complex problems in construction engineering, adapt BIM and digital twin technologies to industry needs, and cultivate an academic environment where graduate students conduct research at international standards.

Research Domains

Artificial Intelligence & GNN

Innovative Graph Neural Network (GNN) methods for modeling project networks, supply chain dynamics, and organizational relationships in construction.

Digital Twin Systems

Flood early warning systems (FLOODTWIN) and seismic digital twin research for critical nuclear power infrastructure with real-time monitoring.

NLP / LLM Decision Support

Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) based legal decision support platforms for construction law, contracts, and regulatory texts.

Building Information Modeling (BIM)

Automation research including natural language BIM parameter labeling (VIBE Framework) and interoperable data integration.

Occupational Safety Analytics

Predictive analytics and computer vision methods to forecast site safety risks and prevent occupational accidents in construction sites.

Lean Construction

Advancing lean construction principles, process optimization, and knowledge transfer to both academia and industry practice.

Digital Construction Laboratory (DCL) conducts research on the digital transformation of the construction industry within ITU Faculty of Civil Engineering.
