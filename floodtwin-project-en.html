Horizon Europe FLOODTWIN Project

Digital Twin Technologies & Flood Early Warning Systems for Urban Infrastructure

Horizon Europe Call: Horizon Europe Cluster 4

Project Scope & Objectives

Coordinated by the Digital Construction Laboratory (DCL) alongside international consortium partners, the FLOODTWIN project proposal has been submitted under the Horizon Europe Cluster 4 (Digital, Industry and Space) call.

The project integrates cyber-physical digital twin models using real-time data feeds to forecast urban flood risks and extreme weather impacts caused by climate change on critical infrastructure.

Technological Innovations

  • IoT & Satellite Data Fusion: Real-time monitoring of river flow rates, precipitation, and soil saturation using IoT sensors and satellite imagery.
  • Structural Health & Seismic Monitoring: Digital twin simulations analyzing structural responses of critical energy and civil infrastructure under compound hazards.
  • AI Decision Support: AI-based hydrodynamic simulations offering instantaneous decision support for local authorities and disaster response teams.
