Open Dataset Released

Bilingual Construction Dispute and Regulation Matching Dataset Available on Zenodo

Open Data DOI: 10.5281/zenodo.20023861

About the Dataset

Researchers at the Digital Construction Laboratory (DCL) have published a bilingual "Construction Dispute and Regulation Matching" dataset as open access on Zenodo as part of their NLP and RAG-based legal AI decision support research.

The dataset maps semantic relationships between construction contract clauses, legal dispute precedents, and public procurement regulation texts, serving as a benchmark dataset for training large language models (LLMs).

Access & Citation

Academic researchers and industry professionals can access the dataset via the Zenodo repository using DOI: 10.5281/zenodo.20023861 under Creative Commons licensing.
